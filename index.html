Our services

Everyday care for the whole family

From a cough that will not clear to your baby's next vaccine, we look after the health needs that matter most, close to home.

  1. General consultations

    Diagnosis, treatment and honest advice from a professional who takes the time to listen.

  2. Blood pressure

    Regular check-ups and ongoing monitoring to keep your blood pressure, and your heart, under control.

  3. Antenatal care

    Caring support and check-ups throughout pregnancy, for a healthy mother and a healthy baby.

  4. Child immunisations

    Keeping your child's vaccinations on schedule. Protect your child and build a healthy future.

  5. Family planning

    Private, respectful guidance on contraception and safe choices for healthier families.

  6. Minor ailments

    Coughs, colds, flu, headaches, stomach upsets and more, treated quickly so you can get back to your day.

Not sure if we can help? Call us or send a WhatsApp message and we will tell you.

Meet our sister

Sister Matsepe

Veromed Private Clinic

Call the clinic WhatsApp

Experience you can trust. Care that feels personal.

Sister Matsepe brings the calm assurance that only many years of nursing can give. Patients know her for her patience, her warmth and her straight, honest advice, and she treats every person who walks through the door with the same respect.

She is especially passionate about women's health: helping women restore their health, their confidence and their sense of wellbeing. Just as important to her is that every woman understands her own body. She regularly teaches women practical ways to monitor their health at home and to recognise early warning signs, so that they can act early and with confidence.

About Veromed

Quality care.
Compassionate service.

Veromed is a private primary health care clinic in the heart of Groblersdal. We believe good health care should be close by, personal and dignified, the kind of care you would want for your own mother or child.
